About us

Cape Cod’s ultimate destination for sun-kissed perfection.

Proudly voted Cape Cod’s #1 tanning center — your go-to tropical escape for over 27+ years.

Welcome to Tanorama of Falmouth — relaxation, rejuvenation, and a personal oasis right here at 365 Main Street.

At the heart of Tanorama is its owner, Scott Boyle — a truly exceptional individual whose warmth, charm, and dedication set the tone for everything we do. Known for his genuine kindness, cool demeanor, and commitment to excellence, he ensures that every visit feels personal and every guest leaves with a smile. Louis is beside him on the floor. Guests write about both of them the same way.

We specialize in creating a personal oasis right here in Falmouth, MA. From our cutting-edge tanning beds and booths featuring five levels of tanning to our flawless spray-tanning services, soothing massages, rejuvenating facials, and transformative red-light therapy, every detail is designed with your comfort and beauty in mind.

Our boutique is stocked with carefully curated products to enhance your style, making it easy to pamper yourself or find the perfect gift for someone special. Plus, with state-of-the-art equipment, premium lamps, and top-tier lotions — all at a place where we care about your experience — your satisfaction is our priority.

Proudly owner-operated, Tanorama is more than just a tanning salon; it’s a place where you’re treated like family. Whether you want to maintain that summer glow, indulge in some “me time,” or simply relax in a friendly, inviting environment, we’re here to make it happen.

Come discover why our customers keep coming back year after year. At Tanorama, we combine beauty, relaxation, and exceptional care — all inspired by the passion and vision of an owner who truly loves what he does. Your year-round oasis awaits.
