Questions we hear on Main Street.

Do I need an appointment?+–

Walk-ins are welcome when a bed is free — that’s how most regulars come. Call or use Contact / Book for spray tan, red light, massage, facials, and busy summer afternoons: (508) 495-9905.

What is red light therapy?+–

Tanorama features the new concept of both healing red light and UV light, which are new to Falmouth. Guests come for skin that looks smoother and more even. It is a wellness service, not a medical treatment.

Will a spray tan look orange?+–

No orange is offered. We use a violet-based Norvell solution in a private digital Auto Revolution spray booth, with nine colors across light, medium, and dark. Custom airbrush is available too.

I’ve never tanned. Where do I start?+–

Tell the front it’s your first visit. Scott Boyle and Louis will walk you through levels, lotion, and time — they will not put you in the most expensive bed just because.

Do you sell packages and gift cards?+–

Yes — tanning packages, memberships, and gift cards are available in the salon. Rates change with the season, so call or ask at the desk for what’s current.

What should I bring?+–

Just you. We have goggles, lotions, and aftercare in the boutique. Arrive with clean, dry skin for spray tan, and skip heavy deodorant or makeup in the booth.
